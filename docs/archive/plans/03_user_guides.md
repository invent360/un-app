## Getting Started

### What is Unetwork?

Unetwork is a mobile app that lets you earn passive income by contributing your phone's connectivity to help telecom companies verify their network quality. Your phone runs small connectivity tests in the background, and you get paid for your contribution.

### How Much Can You Earn?

| Device Type | Monthly Earnings (Your Share) |
|-------------|-------------------------------|
| WiFi-only device (tablet, old phone without SIM) | $2.00-$5.40/month |
| Phone with active SIM (all tasks enabled) | $4.50-$18.00/month |
| Multiple devices | Multiply your earnings! |

*Earnings shown across 50:50 to 60:40 splits. Your actual earnings depend on your split rate.*

### Why the $1.99/Month Fee?

The fee covers operational costs and ensures committed users. You earn $2.00-$18.00/month (depending on performance and split), making it profitable from day one. Earnings are split between you and the operator based on the tier you select.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Getting Started

**Q: How much will I actually earn?**
> Earnings depend on your device type and split rate. Here is what you can expect:
> - **WiFi-only devices**: $2.00-$5.40/month (your share at various splits)
> - **Phone with SIM (typical)**: $4.50-$7.20/month (OK to Good performance)
> - **Phone with SIM (excellent)**: $12.50-$18.00/month (Excellent to Champion performance)
>
> Multiple devices = multiple earnings!

**Q: Why the $1.99/month fee?**
> The fee is paid by the operator (not you!) and covers their costs. You earn $2.00-$18.00/month based on your split rate and device performance. Common splits range from 50:50 to 60:40 (you/operator). Higher first number = more for you!

**Q: How do I withdraw my earnings?**
> Withdraw to BTC, ETH, USDC, or other crypto. Minimum withdrawal is $5.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Can I use an old phone that's not my daily driver?**
> Yes! Any smartphone meeting minimum requirements works. Old phones are perfect - just keep them plugged in and connected to WiFi.

**Q: Does it work on tablets?**
> Partially. Passive tasks (Connection, Connectivity Verification, Entropy) work on WiFi-only tablets. However, Caller ID Testing requires phone capability and SMS Testing requires an active SIM card.

**Q: Will it slow down my phone?**
> No. The app uses minimal CPU (<1%) and runs efficiently in background.

**Q: What if I have limited data?**
> Enable Connection Only mode. Uses ~50-100MB/month - less than watching one Netflix episode.

**Q: Can I turn off tasks temporarily?**
> Yes. Task participation is configurable in app settings (except Connection to Unetwork which is always required).
