## 1. Executive Summary

The UNO (Unetwork License Operator) business model is built on a competitive advantage strategy where the operator pays the $1.99/month subscription fee on behalf of users, providing them with free access to the Unetwork platform. In exchange, earnings are split between the user and operator at competitive rates ranging from 50:50 to 60:40 (user:operator), matching or exceeding the market standard while ensuring operator profitability.

**Key Business Fundamentals:**
- **Total Node Portfolio:** 11 nodes with 2,200 license capacity
- **Subscription Model:** Operator pays $1.99/month per active license
- **User Value Proposition:** Free access + better-than-market earnings split
- **Earnings Range:** $4-30/month per license (8 tiers from Poor to Champion)
- **Target Earnings:** $9-25/month per license for sustainable profitability
- **Primary Market Focus:** Philippines, Pakistan, India, Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana (multi-country strategy)
- **Growth Strategy:** Power user acquisition (20 users x 5 devices vs 100 x 1)

**Revenue Formula:**
```
Net Revenue per License = (Average Earnings x Your Split %) - $1.99 subscription
```

### Core Assumptions - Detailed Breakdown

#### Competitive Splits (50:50 to 60:40)

Our split strategy positions us at or above market rate to attract and retain quality users:

| Split | User Gets | Operator Gets | Use Case | Break-even Earnings |
|-------|-----------|---------------|----------|---------------------|
| **50:50** | 50% | 50% | Standard offer (market rate) | $3.98/mo |
| **52:48** | 52% | 48% | Slightly above market | $4.15/mo |
| **55:45** | 55% | 45% | Power user incentive | $4.42/mo |
| **60:40** | 60% | 40% | Maximum user retention | $4.98/mo |

**Strategy:** Start with 50:50 for general users, offer 55:45 or 60:40 to power users (3+ devices) and early adopters to build loyalty and community advocates.

#### Activation Rate Ramp (50% M1 → 80% M12)

Activation rate = percentage of sign-ups who actually install, configure, and run the app consistently.

| Month | Activation Rate | Reasoning |
|-------|-----------------|-----------|
| M1-M2 | 50% | New users, learning curve, some drop-offs |
| M3-M4 | 55-60% | Improved onboarding, FAQ refinement |
| M5-M6 | 65-70% | Community support kicks in, word-of-mouth quality |
| M7-M9 | 72-75% | Referrals bring pre-qualified users |
| M10-M12 | 78-80% | Optimized funnel, strong community guidance |

**Key Drivers for Improvement:**
- Better onboarding materials and video guides
- Active Discord community answering questions
- Power users helping new users
- Refined targeting attracts more committed users
- Reputation builds trust, reducing "tire kickers"

#### Monthly Churn Rate (8% M1 → 4% M12)

Churn = percentage of active users who stop using the service each month.

| Period | Churn Rate | Active Users Lost (per 1,000) | Reasoning |
|--------|------------|------------------------------|-----------|
| M1-M2 | 8% | 80/month | Early users testing, unrealistic expectations |
| M3-M4 | 6-7% | 60-70/month | Community engagement reduces drop-off |
| M5-M6 | 5-6% | 50-60/month | Users see consistent earnings, trust builds |
| M7-M9 | 4.5-5% | 45-50/month | Strong retention from power users |
| M10-M12 | 4% | 40/month | Loyal community, passive income habit formed |

**Churn Reduction Strategies:**
- Transparent monthly earnings reports
- Active community engagement (Discord events, AMAs)
- Power user recognition and incentives
- Proactive support for users with issues
- Regular communication about platform updates

#### Marketing Budget ($500-$2,000/month)

Budget allocation scales with license utilization and proven ROI:

| Phase | Budget | Allocation | Target |
|-------|--------|------------|--------|
| **M1-M3 (Validation)** | $500-800/mo | 40% influencers, 30% Meta ads, 20% Google, 10% community | 50-150 users |
| **M4-M6 (Growth)** | $800-1,200/mo | 35% influencers, 35% Meta ads, 20% Google, 10% Discord tools | 150-500 users |
| **M7-M9 (Scale)** | $1,200-1,600/mo | 30% influencers, 40% paid ads, 20% retargeting, 10% tools | 500-1,200 users |
| **M10-M12 (Optimize)** | $1,600-2,000/mo | 25% influencers, 45% paid ads, 20% referral bonuses, 10% tools | 1,200-2,000+ users |

**Budget Principles:**
- Never spend more than 30% of monthly net revenue on marketing
- Scale budget only after proving positive unit economics (LTV > CAC)
- Pause and reassess if CPA exceeds $15 for any channel
- Shift budget from underperforming to winning channels monthly

#### CPA - Cost Per Acquisition ($10 M1 → $6 M12)

CPA = total marketing spend ÷ new activated users acquired.

| Period | Target CPA | Realistic Range | How to Achieve |
|--------|------------|-----------------|----------------|
| M1-M2 | $10-12 | $8-15 | Testing channels, expect inefficiency |
| M3-M4 | $9-10 | $7-12 | Identifying winning creatives and audiences |
| M5-M6 | $8-9 | $6-10 | Scaling winners, cutting losers |
| M7-M9 | $7-8 | $5-9 | Referral program contributing 20%+ of sign-ups |
| M10-M12 | $6-7 | $5-8 | Organic + referral = 40%+ of acquisitions |

**CPA Reduction Levers:**
1. **Influencer Optimization:** Double down on creators with <$8 CPA
2. **Referral Program:** Organic referrals have $0 direct cost
3. **Community Word-of-Mouth:** Active Discord = free marketing
4. **Retargeting:** Convert warm leads at lower cost
5. **Organic Content:** Evergreen YouTube/TikTok content from influencers

**Warning Thresholds:**
- CPA > $12: Review targeting and creatives
- CPA > $15: Pause channel, investigate
- CPA > $20: Stop spending, fundamental strategy issue

### Understanding Lease Commitments

> **IMPORTANT: Leases Are Binding**
>
> Once a user activates a license, the lease is bound until the end of the period.
> You cannot change split rates or stop paying the $1.99 subscription mid-lease.
>
> **Implications:**
> - Choose your split rate carefully before launch
> - New split rates only apply to NEW leases
> - Existing users keep their original terms until renewal
> - Plan for worst-case earnings when setting initial splits

### Split Rate Decision Framework

```
+----------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                  SPLIT RATE DECISION TREE                            |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                                                      |
|   AVAILABLE SPLITS: 50:50 | 52:48 | 55:45 | 60:40                    |
|                                                                      |
|   BEFORE LAUNCH: Choose Initial Split                                |
|   |                                                                  |
|   +-- Risk Tolerance: STANDARD                                       |
|   |   --> Use 50:50 split (market rate, best margins)                |
|   |                                                                  |
|   +-- Risk Tolerance: MODERATE (Growth Focus)                        |
|   |   --> Use 52:48 or 55:45 split (slightly above market)           |
|   |                                                                  |
|   +-- Risk Tolerance: HIGH (Max User Value)                          |
|       --> Use 60:40 split (requires $5+/mo earnings)                 |
|                                                                      |
|   AFTER 90 DAYS: Review & Adjust for NEW Leases                      |
|   |                                                                  |
|   +-- If avg earnings > $20/mo                                       |
|   |   --> Offer 55:45 or 60:40 to new users (competitive edge)       |
|   |                                                                  |
|   +-- If avg earnings $12-$20/mo                                     |
|   |   --> Offer 50:50 or 55:45 to new users (balanced approach)      |
|   |                                                                  |
|   +-- If avg earnings $5-$12/mo                                      |
|       --> Keep 50:50 for best margins                                |
|                                                                      |
|   AT RENEWAL: Existing Users                                         |
|   |                                                                  |
|   +-- High-value users (good uptime, referrals)                      |
|   |   --> Upgrade to better split as loyalty reward                  |
|   |                                                                  |
|   +-- Standard users                                                 |
|       --> Offer current rate for new users                           |
|                                                                      |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Monitoring & Adjustment Triggers

Since leases are binding, adjustments happen at these decision points:

| Decision Point | What to Review | Possible Actions |
|----------------|----------------|------------------|
| **Day 30** | Early earnings data | Inform split strategy for next cohort |
| **Day 60** | First users nearing renewal | Decide renewal terms |
| **Day 90** | Solid earnings baseline | Adjust splits for new users only |
| **Each Renewal** | Individual user value | Upgrade high-performers |

### Marketing Your Split Advantage

**For 60:40 Split (Maximum User Value):**
> "Keep 60% of your earnings - 20% more than the market standard. And your subscription? We pay it."

**For 55:45 Split:**
> "Keep 55% of your earnings - 10% more than anywhere else. Zero subscription fees, maximum value."

**For 50:50 Split:**
> "Keep 50% of your earnings - the market standard. And your subscription? We pay it, so you earn from day one."

**For Power Users (55:45 or 60:40):**
> "Run 3+ devices? You're a VIP. Keep up to 60% of earnings + we cover all subscription fees."

### Financial Safeguards

Since you cannot exit leases early, build in these protections:

1. **Conservative First Cohort**: Start with 50:50 for first 50 users
2. **Earnings Validation**: Wait for 30-day earnings data before scaling
3. **Tiered Rollout**: Offer 55:45 after confirming $6+ earnings; only offer 60:40 at $12+ earnings
4. **Power User Priority**: Focus on multi-device users (more revenue per acquisition)
5. **Cash Reserve**: Maintain 3-month subscription buffer ($1.99 x active licenses x 3)

## 8. Power User Strategy

### The 20x5 Model

Instead of acquiring 100 single-device users, target **20 power users with 5 devices each**.

> **Note:** Rewards diminish when multiple devices share the same IP address/LAN. Recommend maximum 5 devices per user to maintain optimal earnings per device.

### Why 20 x 5 Beats 100 x 1

| Metric | 100 Users x 1 Device | 20 Users x 5 Devices |
|--------|---------------------|----------------------|
| **Total licenses** | 100 | 100 |
| **Support tickets/month** | ~50-80 | ~10-15 |
| **Churn risk** | High (casual users) | Low (invested users) |
| **Earnings consistency** | Variable | Stable (power users optimize) |
| **Community engagement** | Low | High (core advocates) |
| **Referral quality** | Random | Targeted (know other power users) |
| **Setup support time** | 100 sessions | 20 sessions |

+----------------------------------------------------------------------+

### Response Plan B: Platform Outage

```
IMMEDIATE:
- Post status update in Discord within 1 hour
- "Unetwork is experiencing issues. This affects all operators, not just us."
- Link to official Unetwork status/Twitter

DURING OUTAGE:
- Update community every 4-6 hours
- DO NOT speculate about causes
- Reassure users their licenses/earnings are safe

AFTER RESOLUTION:
- Post summary of what happened
- If earnings were lost, explain Unetwork's compensation (if any)
- Thank community for patience
```

### Response Plan C: Competitor Beats Your Terms

```
IF competitor offers better split (e.g., 70/30 vs your 60/40):

OPTION 1: Match (if economics allow)
- Announce you're matching the better terms
- Frame as "we always want to be competitive"
- Note: You offer splits from 50:50 to 60:40 (user gets 50-60%)

OPTION 2: Differentiate on value (recommended)
- "They offer 80/20, we offer 50:50 to 60:40 PLUS:"
  - Personal support (not just Discord)
  - Fiat withdrawal assistance
  - Power user community
  - Transparent monthly reports
- "The extra 10% = $0.90/month at $9 earnings. Our support saves you hours."

OPTION 3: Niche down
- Focus on power users only (competitors won't serve them well)
- Focus on one country (become THE option there)
```

### Response Plan D: Burnout Recovery

```
WARNING SIGNS:
- Dreading Discord notifications
- Skipping support responses
- Resentment toward users
- Considering quitting entirely

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
1. Set Discord to "Do Not Disturb" for 48 hours
2. Post: "Taking a brief break for personal reasons. Back in 2 days."
3. Review what's causing the most stress

STRUCTURAL FIXES:
- Recruit volunteer moderators (power users)
- Raise automation level
- Reduce acquisition pace
- Consider: is this the right business for you?

LAST RESORT:
- Transfer operation to another UNO (partnership)
- Sell your community/list to competitor (with user consent)
- Wind down gracefully (see Exit Strategy)
```

### Response Plan E: Crypto Market Crash

```
IF crypto drops 50%+ and user earnings feel worthless:

MESSAGING:
- "Yes, crypto is down. But earnings are still accumulating."
- "Users who held through 2022 bear market are glad they did."
- "This is passive income - it compounds over time."

TACTICAL:
- Emphasize bank withdrawal option (fiat)
- Highlight that network rewards are stable (task-based, not price-based)
- Consider: offer to help users convert to stablecoins
```

### Response Plan F: Regulatory Action (Critical)

```
IF Unetwork faces regulatory shutdown or legal issues:

IMMEDIATE:
- DO NOT make any public statements until you understand the situation
- Consult with legal counsel if available
- Document everything (communications, earnings, user data)

COMMUNICATION:
- Be honest: "Unetwork is facing regulatory challenges. Here's what we know."
- DO NOT promise things you can't control
- Advise users to withdraw earnings if possible

EXIT:
- If Unetwork shuts down, your UNO operation ends
- Help users transition to alternatives if possible
- Pivot Discord community to general DePIN/passive income
```
