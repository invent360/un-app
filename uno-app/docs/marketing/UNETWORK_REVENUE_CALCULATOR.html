UNETWORK · Planning model · Revision 4

Revenue growth after expenses

Editable task-driven forecast for a 2,500-licence portfolio. Begin with the reference scenario or enter your own verified task rates. All amounts are USD; task reward inputs are UP converted by the UP/USD setting. This is a scenario calculator, not a claim about observed per-device earnings.

Default rewards are illustrative. The CSV has no funded licence-day denominator and cannot calibrate these rates. Disable the illustrative combined pool before enabling named tasks. No projected Entropy or GPU revenue is included by default.

1 · Assumptions and costs

Fractions use 0–1 (0.02 = 2%). The forecast begins at production week 1 after validation. New successes join evenly through each week; failed trials consume a fresh credit block. All costs remain fixed when adding a task unless you change its optional extra cost or other controls.

Scenario buttons reset tasks to one illustrative pool at the selected rate. They preserve your non-task assumptions. These are sensitivity levels, not probabilities or empirical expectations.

2 · Plug in tasks

Add one row for each additive task. “Daily reward” is UP per eligible device-day, not per call. For event tasks use reward per accepted event × expected accepted events per eligible device-day. Mix, eligibility and activity factors control effective rewarded devices.

3 · Forecast results

Profit uses earned UNO revenue less all configured costs and full credit blocks purchased in that week. Cash includes the settlement delay. Profit can fluctuate at renewal dates. Funding excludes existing opening cash and is the maximum cumulative cash deficit including setup.

Method and operating limits

Default timing: 2% of opening productive licences churn at each week start; net target is 250/week until capacity. Required successes replace churn and add the target. Gross attempts are rounded up at 85% success. Successful devices join uniformly each day and earn a half-day on arrival. Churned licences stop earning immediately. Failed trials are conservatively charged one full credit block, consume a half-day of support, earn nothing and are released before successful same-day placements. Verify that legal release and support capacity make this feasible; otherwise extend the deployment.

Credits renew on each surviving cohort’s 30-day anniversary and are expensed in full when purchased, with no refunds or transfers assumed. This is a conservative managerial result, not a formal accrual income statement. Support is prorated by actual modelled device-days. Acquisition and coordination switch to maintenance amounts when no further net growth is needed. Settlement lag applies only to the UNO’s net reward receipt; ULO and referral allocations are shown separately, assumed paid directly by the platform and are not UNO costs a second time.

The forecast includes credits, failed trials, renewals, support, recruitment, coordinator pay, additional overhead, task costs, fees, tax provision, setup and optional historic licence-cost allocation. Defaults for taxes, fees, additional overhead and capital allocation are zero because no actual amounts were supplied. Positive-week tax is a conservative simple provision without loss offsets; replace with actual treatment. Historic licence cost is optional noncash allocation, not new acquisition cash. All ordinary modelled costs are paid in the week incurred. Reward redemption at the entered conversion and lag is assumed, not verified.

Default device mix is 100% Android; mix is constant across cohorts. Tasks with several supported device types count their summed mutually exclusive shares once. Country restrictions belong in each task’s eligible fraction. Daily pool caps can represent known limits but are not inferred from the CSV. The model assumes additive tasks; reduce factors if they compete for resources. Default activity = 1 assumes the illustrative daily rate already includes expected task availability. No unlimited task demand is established by that assumption.
